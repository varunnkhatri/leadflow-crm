# LeadFlow design direction

LeadFlow uses a dark editorial / modern Swiss / kinetic neo-brutalist visual system.

- Near-black canvas with warm editorial white.
- Acid-lime accent used sparingly for live signals and calls to action.
- Oversized condensed-looking typography through scale, tracking and line-height rather than a new font dependency.
- Thin grid rules and asymmetric editorial layouts.
- Motion is structural: marquee, signal bars, orbiting rings and subtle entrance animation.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Mobile changes composition rather than simply shrinking desktop layouts.
